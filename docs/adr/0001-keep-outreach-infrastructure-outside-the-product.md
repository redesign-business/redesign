# Keep outreach infrastructure outside the product

The product owns the differentiating work: discovering businesses, storing their public contact details, and creating finished redesign previews. Instantly owns commodity cold-email concerns such as inbox warmup, sending, rotation, follow-ups, delivery, replies, and campaign state; a CRM can be adopted only if real sales work later requires one. Contact-form outreach is the narrow exception: because Instantly does not submit website forms, the product may run the submission using a connected Instantly inbox as the reply identity. A form submission remains outside Instantly campaign analytics, although a resulting message can appear as non-Instantly mail in Unibox. Do not build parallel form-outreach storage or analytics unless that missing information becomes a demonstrated problem. This boundary keeps the codebase focused on the exact distribution problem without rebuilding mature tools or adding speculative sales infrastructure.
